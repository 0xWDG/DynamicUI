# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in DynamicUI welcoming and harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, ability, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Assume good intent while addressing the substance of disagreements.
- Give actionable feedback and accept reasonable feedback gracefully.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information.
- Focus discussions on improving the project and its community.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate
disruption, and sustained unwelcome behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately through the
maintainer contact methods listed in the README. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as
confidentially as reasonably possible.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
